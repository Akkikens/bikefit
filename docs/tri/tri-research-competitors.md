Headline: every triathlon-specific plan app combined (TriDot new+legacy, MOTTIV, Humango, Athletica, 2PEAK) has fewer than 450 US ratings. Runna alone has 35,585 and TrainerRoad 21,439. The tri plan niche has no breakout iOS product.

## Runna (the model to copy)
- Founded 2020/2021 (sources conflict), subscription launch March 2022, by Dom Maskell and Ben Parker, London. https://www.welltodoglobal.com/post/ben-parker-and-dom-maskell-co-founders-of-runna/ and https://runna.notion.site/Careers-at-Runna-8d9ba4280e70489aad1fad2b1a2aaf47
- Growth: ~7,000 paying customers roughly 11 months post launch (https://thenudgegroup.com/blog/interviews/startup-stories-ben-parker-co-founder-head-coach-at-runna); 30x subscription growth Dec 2022 to Dec 2023 per RevenueCat case study (https://www.casestudies.com/company/revenuecat/case-study/how-revenuecat-helped-runna-reach-30x-year-over-year-growth, also https://www-docs.revenuecat.com/customers/runna); founder cited ~30 percent month over month growth. Apple 2024 iPhone App of the Year finalist (https://startups.co.uk/news/runna-acquired-by-strava/).
- Team size history: >120 people across London and Boston around acquisition; a Welcome to the Jungle listing later shows 201-500 (https://app.welcometothejungle.com/companies/Runna). So Runna was NOT a tiny team at exit; it was venture backed (over 8m GBP raised, 5m GBP round led by JamJar in 2023, https://startups.co.uk/news/runna-acquired-by-strava/).
- Strava acquired Runna April 2025, undisclosed sum (https://dcrainmaker.com/2025/04/strava-acquires-runna-thoughts-forward.html, https://www.techradar.com/health-fitness/fitness-apps/what-strava-buying-runna-means-for-users-of-both-fitness-apps-according-to-their-ceos). Runna continues as a separate app. Joint Strava+Runna subscription later launched at $149.99/yr US, annual only (https://marathonhandbook.com/strava-and-runna-launch-joint-subscription/).
- Pricing: Runna standalone has been reported around $119.99/yr vs Strava at $79.99/yr at acquisition time (https://en.ain.ua/2025/04/17/strava-acquires-runna); monthly reported in the $14-20 range by region. Not verified from a Runna first party page in this pass.
- Triathlon: Runna still does NOT do real triathlon. Its "triathlon plans" cover the run leg only, no swim or bike sessions (Runna help center: https://support.runna.com/en/articles/15443877-how-to-create-a-training-plan-in-runna). No duathlon plans ("We don't offer duathlon plans yet"). A cofounder is quoted saying they skipped triathlon because "it's very complicated to do" (https://the5krunner.com/runna/). No cycling or multisport launch found as of Nov 2026.
- What made it grow (per sources above): one sport done extremely well, personalized plans with real coach voice, Apple Watch execution, pace-based structured workouts, aggressive influencer and community marketing, RevenueCat paywall iteration.

## TriDot
- Pricing (tridot.com/pricing, fetched 2026-11-06, https://www.tridot.com/pricing): Lifestyle $14.99/mo (1 Sprint or Olympic race), Essentials $39/mo (up to 3 races any distance), Complete $99/mo (unlimited races, strength, swim drill optimization), Premium Coached typically $249/mo (dedicated human coach, coaches set own rates). Monthly prices only on page.
- AI claims: markets itself as "the first AI-driven endurance training platform"; new FitLogic engine billed as "the world's first and only AI-native platform developed specifically for endurance athletes," trained on "20+ years of training and race data," and "doesn't just use AI, it is AI" (https://www.tridot.com/pricing). Company claims, no independent verification.
- Ironman partnership: announced Dec 2023 as multi-year "Official Training Platform of IRONMAN" (https://www.endurancesportswire.com/tridot-and-ironman-join-forces-to-provide-athletes-an-unparalleled-training-experience/). TriDot terminated it abruptly in early August 2025 citing "unresolved business matters"; TriDot stopped operating IRONMAN U and launched its own ESCI coach certification; Ironman said the move "came as a surprise to us" (https://slowtwitch.com/industry/lessons-from-the-ironman-x-tridot-break-up/). The Pricing page today contains no Ironman branding.
- Complaints: Slowtwitch posters say Reddit feedback is "a mixed bag" with many favoring the old $9.99 plan, and that TriDot threads are heavy with employees and ambassadors advocating (https://forum.slowtwitch.com/t/triathlon-ai-training-thoughts-tridot-humango-athletica-2peaks-triq/831893). Perceived as expensive; full-featured tiers approach $99/mo. MOTTIV marketing attacks its price (https://www.mymottiv.com/compare/mottiv-vs-tridot). TriDot shipped a brand new iOS app (ID 6754208848, only 34 ratings) while "Legacy TriDot" (263 ratings) still lists, which suggests a rocky app migration.
- Not a solo product: Predictive Fitness, Inc., established company with coach network.

## MOTTIV (closest to "Runna for triathlon")
- Founder: Taren Gesell ("Triathlon Taren," ~200k YouTube subscribers), Calgary, founded 2020; team size reported 9-10 people (https://builtincalgary.org/company/mottiv, https://bouncewatch.com/company/mottiv); pre-seed Jan 2021, seed Jan 2022, amounts undisclosed. App originally built by outside studio EltexSoft (https://eltexsoft.com/cases/mottiv/).
- Pricing (mymottiv.com/pricing, fetched 2026-11-06, https://www.mymottiv.com/pricing): Free plan at $0/mo (personalized plans, strength, nutrition tips, one race at a time, workout ads, NO device/app connections); Premium $19.99/mo or $179.99/yr (from $14.99/mo billed annually): unlimitedited races, Garmin/Strava connections, no ads, support. 30 day money back guarantee. Marketing line: "Why PAY $200-$1000/month FOR a 1-1 coach?" They cut subscription price 65 percent at one point and added the free tier (https://www.endurancesportswire.com/mottiv-training-app-reduces-subscription-fee-by-65/, https://www.endurancesportswire.com/mottiv-announces-free-version-of-their-app-with-new-a-basic-plan/).
- Sports: running, triathlon, cycling, swimrun, duathlon; positions for beginners and intermediate (https://www.active.com/running/articles/mottiv-app-review).
- Traction signals: only 71 US ratings (4.80) after 5+ years; CA store 27 ratings (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1561001166). Strong YouTube funnel but tiny app footprint. This is the single most important datapoint: the loudest "Runna for triathlon" play has not broken out, likely because device sync is paywalled and the app experience trails Runna polish.

## Humango
- HumanGO: AI Training Planner, iOS app ID 1554430755, 21 US ratings (4.52). In-app purchases listed: Endurance annual $169.99, Fitness annual $79.99 (https://apps.apple.com/app/id1554430755). A 2024 review lists four tiers up to ~$29/mo All-Star with triathlon plans for Olympic, half, full (https://fueledbylolz.com/2024/08/28/humango-review/). A competitor page lists Essential $16.99/mo and triathlon requiring Premium $28.99/mo (https://www.gneta.app/compare/humango); conflicting, check vendor.
- Multi-year partnership with Challenge Family race series announced (https://www.challenge-family.com/challenge-family/news/humango-challenge-family-announce-multi-year-agreement/), i.e. it grabbed the number 2 race brand after TriDot had Ironman.
- Complaints: forum users called its "Hugo" AI assistant "laughably bad and pretty much useless" though the product is easy to use (https://forum.athletica.ai/t/athletica-vs-humango-vs-tridot/1930, hosted by competitor Athletica, weigh accordingly). Triathlete.com included it in an age-grouper test and praised wellness-data-driven plan personalization (https://www.triathlete.com/gear/tech-wearables/ai-triathlon-training-apps).
- Small startup (humango Inc.), exact headcount not found.

## Athletica.ai
- Pricing (athletica.ai/pricing, fetched 2026-11-06, https://athletica.ai/pricing/): $19.90/mo, $99/6mo, $189/yr, 2 week trial without credit card. Sports: triathlon, duathlon, running, cycling, rowing, HYROX. AI Coach built with "world-leading physiologists" (Paul Laursen is the known science lead per community discussion), notable stance: "The AI Coach does not automatically modify your training," it explains and suggests.
- iOS app: 41 US ratings, 4.22 average, the weakest rating of the group. Praised on Slowtwitch for scientific transparency (https://forum.slowtwitch.com/t/triathlon-ai-training-thoughts-tridot-humango-athletica-2peaks-triq/831893); also called "expensive compared to others for the same level of information/support" by a forum poster (https://forum.athletica.ai/t/athletica-vs-humango-vs-tridot/1930). Tiny team product.

## 80/20 Endurance (Matt Fitzgerald / David Warden)
- Static plan catalog, not an adaptive app. Individual tri plans: Sprint from $54.95, Olympic from $64.95, Half from $89.95, Full from $99.95; strength add-ons $27.95-$29.95; Team 80/20 membership $16.58/mo paid annually including all plans plus TrainingPeaks Premium (https://www.8020endurance.com/training-plans/sprint-triathlon/, /olympic-triathlon/, /half-distance-triathlon/, /full-distance-triathlon/, team pricing noted on those pages). Older FinalSurge listing showed $29.95/mo or $199/yr, likely stale (https://www.finalsurge.com/club/team8020/plan/75425).
- Plans also sold through the TrainingPeaks marketplace with free sample versions (e.g. https://www.trainingpeaks.com/training-plans/triathlon/ironman/tp-141386/80-20-triathlon-2026-edition-free-sample-full-distance-level-2-pace-and-power-fr). Essentially a 2 person content business riding other platforms; proof that trusted methodology sells without an app.

## TrainingPeaks (the incumbent for serious triathletes)
- Premium for athletes: annual rose $124.99 to $134.99 USD effective April 2, 2025 (official help center: https://help.trainingpeaks.com/hc/en-us/articles/12916774026765-TrainingPeaks-Annual-Premium-Subscription-Pricing-Update); monthly ~$19-19.95 per third party and ad landing pages, unverified (https://www.trainingpeaks.com/pricing/for-athletes/). 12,770 US ratings (4.70).
- Moat: the coach ecosystem (coach subscriptions, coach-priced tiers up to ~$359/mo; https://trainingpeaks.com/pricing/for-coaches) and the plan marketplace where thousands of coach-authored tri plans sell as one-time purchases (e.g. 70.3 plans from free to ~$100+, https://www.trainingpeaks.com/training-plans/triathlon). Virtually every serious age grouper with a human coach lives here. Complaints: dated UX, "free until you want the feature you really need" (https://beginnertriathlete.com/discussion/forums/thread-view.asp?tid=555931), intimidating for beginners.
- Not beatable head-on for data nerds with coaches; beatable on mobile-first experience and beginner friendliness.

## JOIN Cycling
- Adaptive cycling plans, Dutch company JOIN Sports B.V., raised 1.5m EUR Jan 2024 (https://www.eu-startups.com/2024/01/amsterdam-based-cycling-app-join-gets-e1-5-million-to-further-product-development-and-uk-us-expansion/). 364 US ratings (4.73). Pricing conflicts: App Store monthly $22.99; join.cc campaign page 16.99 EUR/mo (https://join.cc/campaigns/pricing-campaign).
- JOIN Running launched Jan 2025 as cross-training (https://join.cc/cycling-tips/join-cycling-app-launches-join-running-a-strategic-cross-training-feature-for-cyclists). Official help center: "we do not yet offer full triathlon training plans"; swimming can only be logged and counts less toward load (https://help.join.cc/hc/en-150/articles/6543382552593-Triathlons-JOIN). So even the best adaptive cycling app has not closed the tri gap.

## Zwift and TrainerRoad (adjacency, not competitors for plans)
- Zwift: $19.99/mo after 2024 increase, annual $199.99 (https://dcrainmaker.com/2024/05/increases-prices-hardware.html, https://www.zwift.com/uk/news/33635-how-much-does-zwift-cost-and-whats-included-everything-you-need-to-know). 21,480 US ratings. Indoor bike entertainment; triathletes use it for bike sessions.
- TrainerRoad: $21.99/mo, $209.99/yr (https://join.cc/ride-smarter/trainerroad, forum price threads https://www.trainerroad.com/forum/t/tr-price-increase-to-189-95-year-19-95-month/18551). 21,439 US ratings. Has tri plans but is bike-centric; users have asked for duathlon integration for years (https://www.trainerroad.com/forum/t/still-no-duathlon-plans/71091.md). An app that schedules swim/run around Zwift or TrainerRoad bike workouts is a real use case.

## Garmin Coach / Garmin free plans
- Garmin added Garmin Coach triathlon plans (free with a Garmin watch) around the Fenix 8 era; per a Garmin forum post the longest tri plan tops out at OLYMPIC distance, found on Connect web under Training and Planning (https://forums.garmin.com/outdoor-recreation/outdoor-recreation/f/descent-mk3-series/422830/triathlon-training-program; user generated, verify in Connect). No free Garmin 70.3 or full plan found. So the free incumbent stops exactly where the 70.3 market starts.

## Apple Watch native support
- watchOS 9 (2022) added the Multisport workout type: auto-detects swim/bike/run transitions, manual transitions option added in beta 5, still present in current watchOS (Apple user guide: https://support.apple.com/en-mn/guide/watch/combine-multiple-workouts-apdf42f22a41/11.0/watchos/11.0; https://dcrainmaker.com/2022/06/triathlon-features-detailed.html; https://dcrainmaker.com/2022/08/triathlon-transitions-watchos.html). Apple provides the race-day tracking primitive but NO triathlon training plans. HealthKit exposes multisport workouts an app can read.

## Team size scorecard (solo/tiny vs funded)
- Tiny: MOTTIV (~9-10), Athletica (small science-led team), Humango (small startup), 80/20 (content duo), 2PEAK, CLASH Endurance.
- Funded/large: Runna (120+ at exit, now Strava), TriDot/Predictive Fitness, TrainingPeaks (Peaksware), Zwift, TrainerRoad, JOIN (1.5m EUR seed).
- Conclusion: everything tri-specific is tiny, and none has Runna-grade product polish or an Apple Watch first experience.

## Complaint themes (forums/reviews; direct Reddit threads did not surface via search engines in this pass, Slowtwitch and review sites used instead)
- TriDot: price, pushy ambassador marketing, app migration churn (https://forum.slowtwitch.com/t/triathlon-ai-training-thoughts-tridot-humango-athletica-2peaks-triq/831893).
- Humango: weak AI chat assistant, tier confusion (same thread; https://fueledbylolz.com/2024/08/28/humango-review/).
- Athletica: cost vs features, modest app ratings (4.22) despite good science (https://forum.athletica.ai/t/athletica-vs-humango-vs-tridot/1930).
- MOTTIV: device sync locked behind paywall on the free tier (own pricing page), tiny reach.
- TrainingPeaks: complexity, nickel-and-diming perception, intimidating to first timers (https://beginnertriathlete.com/discussion/forums/thread-view.asp?tid=555931).
- General: nobody owns "first triathlon" onboarding the way Couch to 5K / Runna owns first 5K.

## Unserved niches and the wedge
1. Beginner first-timers (sprint/Olympic): Garmin is free but requires a Garmin watch and stops at Olympic; MOTTIV free tier is ad-laden and sync-less; TriDot entry tier is $14.99/mo for one short race. A genuinely FREE, polished, Apple Watch first sprint/Olympic plan app has no competitor at all.
2. First 70.3: the single highest-intent moment in the sport (people sign up 6-9 months out, are scared, and currently get pushed to a $50-100 static TrainingPeaks plan or a $39-99/mo TriDot sub). No free option exists anywhere.
3. Apple Watch first athletes: every incumbent is Garmin-centric; Apple has the multisport workout type since watchOS 9 but no plan layer. An app that writes the day's swim/bike/run to the Watch as guided workouts (HKWorkoutSession, which Akshay already built for Liftspark) and reads multisport race data back is an open lane.
4. Data nerds are served (TrainingPeaks, Athletica, WKO) and locked in; do not chase them for 1.0.
5. Free is a differentiator no VC-funded competitor can match, and the no revenue constraint forces it anyway. Precedent: MOTTIV added a free tier defensively, but crippled it (no device sync, ads).

## Where a free, iPhone native, Apple Watch first app wedges in
- Position: "Your first triathlon, free." Sprint, Olympic, and first 70.3 plans, adaptive scheduling, Watch guided workouts for all three sports, HealthKit-native (Data Not Collected is a believable App Privacy story because no backend account is required for plans).
- Reuse: HR BLE plugin, HKWorkoutSession Watch app, Live Activity, TRIMP/zones engine from Liftspark; the reviewed bike fit camera spec becomes a unique differentiator none of these apps has (TriDot/MOTTIV/Humango have zero bike fit tooling).
- Avoid for 1.0: full Ironman distance (liability of expectations), human coach marketplace, Strava-style social graph (Strava API terms plus solo scale make a community clone impractical; a share-card plus club/leaderboard-lite is enough).
- The market structure is favorable: Runna proved the plan-app playbook and then exited to Strava without ever doing swim/bike; every tri-specific attempt is tiny, paywalled, and Garmin-first.

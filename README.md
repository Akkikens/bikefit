# Saddlemark (working name)

On device bike fit for iPhone: record a side view while you pedal on a trainer, get your joint angles with error bars, a saddle height starting point, and a reference you can check against whenever your bike or body changes. Nothing leaves the phone. Free.

Status: spec only. No Xcode project yet.

- Spec: [docs/specs/bikefit-spec.md](docs/specs/bikefit-spec.md)
- Research notes behind the spec (fit science, Apple Vision, competitors, market, risk, protocol, the brainstorm angles, the synthesis and the critic): [docs/research](docs/research)

Hard rules carried over from Akshay's other apps: free, no revenue for now (StoreKit only behind an OFF Info.plist flag), App Privacy Data Not Collected, no third party SDKs, sole author commits, no em or en dashes in user facing copy.
